🗣️

Language

English is the official language. Ghana is also home to many widely spoken local languages including Akan, Ewe, Ga and Dagbani.

💰

Currency

Ghana uses the Ghanaian cedi (GHS). The cedi is divided into 100 pesewas.

🌊

Coastline

Ghana has a coastline along the Gulf of Guinea, part of the Atlantic Ocean.

☀️

Climate

Ghana has a tropical climate, with temperatures generally remaining warm throughout the year.

Geography & Nature

🗺️

Location

Ghana is located in West Africa. It borders Côte d’Ivoire, Burkina Faso and Togo, while the Gulf of Guinea lies to the south.

🏔️

Highest Point

Mount Afadja, also known as Afadjato, rises to about 885 metres and is located near Ghana's border with Togo.

🌊

Lake Volta

Lake Volta is one of the world's largest artificial lakes by surface area and covers a huge part of eastern Ghana.

🐘

Wildlife

Ghana protects forests, savannahs and wetlands that provide habitats for elephants, monkeys, antelopes and many bird species.

Welcome to Accra

A fast-growing coastal city where history, business and modern African culture meet.

🏙️

Capital

Accra is Ghana's capital and one of the country's main economic, political and cultural centres.

🌴

Coastal City

Accra sits on Ghana's Atlantic coast and combines beaches, markets, restaurants and busy urban districts.

🎨

Creative Scene

Music, fashion, film, art and food have made Accra an influential cultural hub in modern West Africa.

Ghana's History

Before European rule

Powerful African Kingdoms

The region was home to powerful states and trading societies. The Asante Empire later became one of the most influential political powers in the area.

15th century

European Contact

Portuguese traders reached the coast and established trading connections. European powers later competed for control of coastal trade.

1821–1957

British Gold Coast

The territory became known as the Gold Coast under British rule. The name reflected the region's long association with gold.

6 March 1957

Independence 🇬🇭

Ghana became independent from British colonial rule. Kwame Nkrumah became the country's first prime minister.

1960

Republic of Ghana

Ghana became a republic, with Kwame Nkrumah serving as its first president.

Gold, Cocoa & Business

🥇

Gold

Gold has been central to Ghana's economy and history for centuries. Ghana remains one of Africa's major gold-producing countries.

🍫

Cocoa

Ghana is one of the world's major cocoa producers. Cocoa farming supports millions of livelihoods and supplies the global chocolate industry.

🛢️

Oil & Gas

Ghana also produces petroleum and natural gas from offshore fields in the Gulf of Guinea.

📈

Growing Economy

Important sectors include agriculture, mining, manufacturing, services, finance, technology and tourism.

Culture & Traditions

🥁

Music

Ghana has influenced African music through styles such as Highlife and Hiplife. Modern Afrobeats has also become globally popular.

👗

Kente

Kente is a colourful woven textile strongly associated with Ghanaian heritage, especially among Akan communities.

🍲

Food

Popular dishes include jollof rice, fufu, banku, waakye, kenkey and red-red.

🎉

Festivals

Festivals such as Homowo, Akwasidae and Hogbetsotso celebrate history, community, traditions and ancestors.

Football & the Black Stars

⚽

Ghana's National Team

The Ghana national football team is nicknamed the Black Stars, inspired by the Black Star of Africa. Ghana has won the Africa Cup of Nations four times and has become one of Africa's best-known football nations.

4×

AFCON Champions

Ghana has won the Africa Cup of Nations four times.

2006

World Cup Debut

Ghana qualified for its first FIFA World Cup in Germany.

2010

Historic Run

Ghana reached the quarter-finals of the World Cup in South Africa.

Did You Know?

⭐

The Black Star

The black star on Ghana's flag represents African freedom and the country's role in the Pan-African movement.

🌍

Pan-African Symbol

Ghana became an important symbol of African independence after becoming the first sub-Saharan African colony to gain independence in the post-war era.

🍫

Chocolate Connection

Cocoa is one of Ghana's most important agricultural exports and is a key ingredient in chocolate produced around the world.

🏰

Historic Forts

Ghana's coastline contains historic forts and castles connected to centuries of European trade and the transatlantic slave trade.

🇬🇭

AKWAABA

“Akwaaba” is a commonly used Ghanaian expression meaning “Welcome.”
