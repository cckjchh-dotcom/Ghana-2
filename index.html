Ghana 🇬🇭

A country in West Africa

Presentation – 9th Grade

📍 General Information

  • Capital: Accra
  • Continent: Africa
  • Language: English
  • Currency: Ghanaian Cedi
  • Population: about 35 million

🌍 Location

🌍
  • Ghana is in West Africa
  • It is next to the Atlantic Ocean
  • Neighboring countries:
  • Togo, Burkina Faso and Ivory Coast

🏙️ The Capital: Accra

  • Accra is the capital city
  • It is the biggest city in Ghana
  • It is located near the ocean
  • It is an important business center

🌴 Nature and Climate

  • Ghana has a tropical climate
  • It is warm throughout the year
  • Rainforests are found in the south
  • Savannas are found in the north

💰 Economy

  • Important products: cocoa, gold and oil
  • Ghana produces a lot of cocoa
  • Cocoa is used to make chocolate

🍲 Food and Culture

  • Jollof rice is very popular
  • Fufu is also a traditional food
  • Music and dancing are important
  • Ghana has many different traditions

⚽ Sports

  • Football is very popular
  • The national team is called the "Black Stars" ⭐
  • Ghana has played at several World Cups

📜 History

  • Ghana was a British colony
  • Ghana became independent in 1957
  • Kwame Nkrumah was the first prime minister
  • Later, he became the first president
