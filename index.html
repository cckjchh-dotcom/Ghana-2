01 — QUICK FACTS

Ghana at a glance

🏙️

Capital

Accra is the capital and one of the biggest cities in Ghana.

🌍

Location

Ghana is located in West Africa, directly next to the Atlantic Ocean.

🗣️

Language

English is the official language. Many local languages are also spoken.

💰

Currency

People use the Ghanaian Cedi as their national currency.

02 — GEOGRAPHY

Where is Ghana?

🌍

Ghana is located in West Africa. It has a coastline along the Atlantic Ocean.

Ghana shares borders with Togo, Burkina Faso and Ivory Coast.

The country has beaches, forests, rivers and large areas of savanna.

03 — THE CAPITAL

Accra 🏙️

🌊

By the ocean

Accra lies on the coast of the Gulf of Guinea.

👥

Large population

Accra is a busy city where many people live and work.

💼

Business

The city is an important center for business, trade and education.

🏛️

Government

Ghana's government and many important institutions are located here.

04 — NATURE

Nature & Climate 🌴

🌳

Rainforests

Tropical forests can be found mainly in the southern part of Ghana.

🌾

Savanna

The north is much drier and has large areas of grassland and savanna.

☀️

Warm climate

Ghana has a tropical climate and temperatures are usually warm.

🌧️

Rainy seasons

Rainfall changes depending on the region and the time of year.

05 — ECONOMY

What does Ghana produce? 💰

🍫

Cocoa

Cocoa is one of Ghana's most important agricultural products. It is used to make chocolate.

🥇

Gold

Gold mining is an important part of Ghana's economy and exports.

🛢️

Oil

Ghana also produces oil, especially from fields located offshore.

🌾

Agriculture

Farming is important and provides jobs for many people.

06 — CULTURE

Food & Culture 🍲

🍚

Jollof Rice

A popular rice dish cooked with tomatoes, spices and vegetables.

🥣

Fufu

A traditional food made from ingredients such as cassava or plantain.

🥁

Music & Dance

Music, drums and dancing are important parts of Ghanaian culture.

07 — HISTORY

A short history 📜

Before 1957

Ghana was known as the Gold Coast and was controlled by Britain.

1957

Ghana became independent on March 6, 1957.

Kwame Nkrumah

He became the country's first Prime Minister and later its first President.

Today

Ghana is an independent country with a democratic government.

08 — SPORTS

Football ⚽

⚽

Football is the most popular sport in Ghana.

The national team is called the Black Stars.

Ghana has represented Africa at several FIFA World Cups.

Many young people dream of becoming professional football players.
